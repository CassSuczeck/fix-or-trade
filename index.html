      Important: this is an estimate, not an offer

      This tool gives a rough, directional comparison. It's based on general depreciation and upkeep patterns and on what you enter. It is not a vehicle appraisal, a trade-in or purchase offer, a written repair estimate, a credit offer, or financial or legal advice. Condition, trim, options, history, market conditions and your credit all change the real numbers. Before you decide, get a written trade-in offer from a dealer and a written repair estimate.

      Costs not included

      The "trade in & replace" figure does not include New Jersey sales tax (currently 6.625%), NJ MVC title and registration fees, dealer documentary or other fees, insurance, or any loan balance you still owe on your current car. Those costs vary by vehicle, dealer and driver, and they will make buying a vehicle cost more than shown here. Insurance and registration can differ a lot between your current car and a newer one, so ask your insurer and the dealer for exact figures.

      Financing

      Payments calculated from a price, APR and term are for illustration only. Your actual rate and terms depend on your lender and credit approval.

      Rust, safety & recalls

      Only a hands-on inspection can confirm rust damage or whether a car is safe to drive. If you suspect frame or brake-line rust, have it inspected before you spend money on other repairs. Check open recalls for free at nhtsa.gov/recalls (opens in a new tab).
